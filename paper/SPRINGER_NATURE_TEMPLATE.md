# Springer Nature Template

The files `sn-jnl.cls` and `sn-nature.bst` are copied without modification
from the official Springer Nature journal article LaTeX template, version
3.1 (December 2024):

<https://www.springernature.com/gp/authors/campaigns/latex-author-support>

`main.tex` uses the `referee`, `lineno`, `pdflatex`, and `sn-nature` class
options for a reviewer-readable Nature Portfolio submission.
